1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

1.1 The bidder must possess a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) issued by the
Income Tax Department of India. A self-attested copy of the PAN card must be
submitted along with the bid.

1.2 The bidder must be registered under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act and
must submit a valid GST registration certificate as proof.

1.3 The bidder must be registered as a Micro, Small or Medium Enterprise (MSME)
under the Udyam Registration portal, if claiming MSME benefits under this tender.
Udyam Registration Certificate must be enclosed.

[PAGE 3]
2. FINANCIAL CRITERIA

2.1 The bidder must have a minimum average annual turnover of Rs. 5 crore (Rupees
Five Crore only) during the last three (3) financial years (2022-23, 2023-24,
2024-25), as evidenced by audited financial statements / Chartered Accountant
certified turnover certificate.

2.2 The bidder shall submit Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last three financial
years as proof of financial standing.

[PAGE 4]
3. DOCUMENT AND DECLARATION REQUIREMENTS

3.1 The bidder must not be currently blacklisted or debarred by any Central/State
Government department, PSU, or GeM. A self-declaration to this effect, on the
bidder's letterhead, must be submitted with the bid.

3.2 Bidders registered as Startups recognized by DPIIT are exempted from the
Prior Turnover and Prior Experience criteria, subject to submission of a valid
DPIIT Startup recognition certificate.

3.3 EPFO registration certificate must be submitted if the bidder employs 20 or
more persons, as applicable under the Employees' Provident Fund Act.

[PAGE 5]
4. TECHNICAL / PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

4.1 All supplied valves must conform to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)
certification IS 780 for sluice valves, and a valid BIS license copy must be
enclosed.

4.2 Preference under the "Make in India" policy shall be given to bidders offering
products with a minimum of 50% local content, as self-certified by the bidder in
the format prescribed under the Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India)
Order.
